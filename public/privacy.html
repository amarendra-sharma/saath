Saath Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026 · Saath is provided by MacroNations LLC, New York, USA

The short version

  • Saath helps families and friends share costs. What you add to a circle (bills, receipts, payments, payment details) is visible only to the members of that circle.
  • We collect only what the app needs to work. No advertising, no tracking, no analytics SDKs, and we never sell or rent your data.
  • Saath never moves money. When you pay, you use your own payment app (UPI, Wise, bank and so on), and that provider's privacy policy applies.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete my account.

1. Who we are

"Saath", "we" and "us" mean MacroNations LLC, a New York limited liability company, which runs the Saath mobile and web app. MacroNations LLC is the data controller for personal information processed through Saath. You can reach us at privacy@macronations.com.

Device permissions

Saath asks for the camera and photo library only when you choose to photograph or attach a receipt or payment screenshot. It does not use your location, contacts, microphone or advertising identifier.

Sensitive information

Bills and receipts for medical care can reveal health information about the person being cared for. Upload only what your circle needs. This information is visible only to members of that circle and is never used for any other purpose.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the app: calculating each person's share, balances and statements, and showing circle activity.
  • To send reminders and service messages by WhatsApp or email, such as dues reminders and password-reset codes, when these are enabled.
  • To keep Saath secure, prevent misuse and fix problems.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or automated decisions about you, and we do not use circle content to train AI models.

Legal bases, where they apply (for example under the GDPR): performing our contract with you (providing the app), your consent (optional reminders and device permissions, which you can withdraw at any time), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

4. Who can see your information

  • Members of your circle can see everything in that circle: members, shares, balances, bills, receipts, payments, receiver payment details and activity. Only organisers can see the reminder log. People outside a circle cannot see it.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf, only to run Saath:
    • Supabase (database, authentication, file storage and server functions; hosted in the United States)
    • Resend (sending email)
    • Meta Platforms / WhatsApp Business (sending WhatsApp reminders, if enabled; WhatsApp receives the recipient's number and the message text)
  • Authorities, if the law requires it, or to protect the safety, rights or property of people or of Saath.

We never sell, rent or trade personal information.

Payment apps

When you tap "Open UPI", "Open Wise" or a similar button, Saath opens that provider's app or website with details filled in. Saath does not receive, hold or transfer money and never sees your banking login. The provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to the payment.

Shared sign-in with MacroNations

Saath uses the same sign-in system as the MacroNations learning platform, so one login works for both. Saath circle data and MacroNations course data are kept separate, and neither service can read the other's data.

5. How long we keep information

  • Circle content stays until it is deleted by the person who added it or by an organiser, or until the circle is removed.
  • Password-reset codes expire after 15 minutes and are stored only as a hash.
  • When you delete your account, we remove your login and profile, and anonymise your membership in every circle: your name, phone and email are replaced with "Former member". Bills and payments you recorded stay in your circles as anonymised records, so the shared accounts remain correct for the rest of the family. If you were the only person on the app in a circle, that circle is deleted entirely.
  • Backups kept by our hosting provider are overwritten on a rolling basis.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our hosting provider. Database access is limited by row-level security rules, so each request can only reach circles the user belongs to. Receipts are kept in private storage and shown through links that expire after one hour. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell affected users about any breach as the law requires.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct: edit your name, phone number, language and reminder settings in Profile. Organisers can edit circle content.
  • Turn off reminders: switch off WhatsApp or email reminders in Profile or in your circle details.
  • Delete: Profile → Delete my account. You can also leave any circle.
  • Export: any member can export a circle statement as PDF or CSV.
  • If someone added you without your agreement: email us and we will remove or anonymise your details.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU or UK under the GDPR, India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may also have the right to request a copy of your data, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, and complain to your local data-protection authority. Email privacy@macronations.com and we will respond within 30 days. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights.

8. International transfers

Saath is used by families in many countries, and our service providers process data in the United States and elsewhere. Where the law requires, these transfers are protected by safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

9. Children

Saath is meant for adults managing shared expenses. It is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes we will also tell users in the app or by email before they take effect.
