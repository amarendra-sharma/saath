    How to use Bengu

    Bengu helps a group share costs over weeks, months or years. This guide follows the app screen by screen, in the order you'll meet them.

    Guide updated 12 November 2026 · matches the current app · What's new

    1Getting started

    Bengu works in any web browser at bengu.macronations.com, on a phone or a computer.

    1. Open Bengu and choose Create account. Enter your name, email and a password. If you already use a MacroNations course, your MacroNations sign-in works here too.
    2. Pick your language. You can change it any time in Profile.
    3. On the home screen, choose Create a circle to start one, or Join if someone sent you an invite code.
    A circle is one shared budget: one group of people, one currency, one set of bills. You can be in as many circles as you like.

    2Create a circle

    The organiser sets the circle up in three short steps.

    1. What it's for. Give it a name (for example "Papa's care" or "Goa trip") and pick a purpose: medical care, elder care, education, household, trip, event, project, club or community, fundraiser, funeral or memorial, pets, or other. The purpose decides which bill categories you'll see. Choose the currency and, if you like, the city.
    2. How costs are shared.
      • Split each bill: every bill you add is divided by each person's percentage. Best when costs vary.
      • Fixed amount each period: a regular amount (for example ₹60,000 a month for a nurse) that each person pays their percentage of every period. Bills are still recorded so everyone can see them, but they don't change what anyone owes.
    3. When to settle. Choose weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly or quarterly, and the due day. Turn on Organiser confirms payments if a payment should count only once the organiser confirms the money arrived.
    Tip: you can change the period, due day and split style later in Settings. Changes apply to new bills and periods from then on.

    3Invite people and set shares

    Inviting

    1. In the circle, open People and choose Invite family. Send the message on WhatsApp or anywhere else. It contains a 7-letter invite code.
    2. The other person opens Bengu, chooses Join and enters the code. If you already added them by name, they pick their name so they keep their share and history.

    You can also add people who will never install the app (for example an older relative). Add their phone number or email and they still get reminders.

    Shares

    Open Shares and set each person's percentage. They can be anything the group agrees on and don't have to be equal; Split equally sets equal shares in one tap. The total must be 100%. Bengu warns you if it isn't.

    New shares apply to bills added from then on. Past bills keep the split they had.

    4Who receives the money

    Tell Bengu who collects the money and how people can pay them. This is usually one family member who pays the hospital, or the hospital itself.

    1. Open Settings → Who receives the money, or tap Add payment details on the Pay screen.
    2. Add the receiver. If they are a member, link the member who actually uses Bengu (shown with a phone icon). Only then can they record what they pay out in the Money trail.
    3. Add one or more payment methods: UPI ID, Indian or international bank account, Wise, PayPal, Revolut, Zelle, Venmo, Cash App, SEPA (IBAN), M-Pesa, Pix, Remitly or cash.
    UPI IDs look like name@bank (for example 98xxxxxx10@ybl). Without the part after @, payment apps can't open it, so Bengu warns you.

    If a hospital or provider should be paid directly, add it as a separate receiver. Money paid straight to the hospital never sits with one person.

    5Adding bills

    1. In the circle, tap Add a bill.
    2. Read it with AI (optional): take a photo or choose a PDF, and Bengu fills in the amount, date, seller and category for you to check. A multi-page PDF with several bills is split into separate bills. See Bengu Plus.
    3. Or type it in: title, amount, date, category and seller.
    4. Attach the receipt (photo or PDF). Bills without a receipt are marked so the group can see what's missing.
    5. Under Who paid?, leave the receiver selected if the bill is paid from the group's money. If you paid it from your own pocket, choose I paid (or the member who did): they are credited automatically and the others owe them their part.
    6. To split one bill differently (for example one person covers it alone), use Split this bill differently. The split must add up to 100%.
    Always check AI readings. Bengu highlights anything it wasn't sure about. Amounts are never guessed: an unreadable amount is left blank for you to fill in.

    Each bill in the list shows whether it has been paid to the provider: Paid ✓ (verified), Paid, Part paid or Not marked paid.

    6Paying your share

    Your balance is at the top of the circle. Red means you owe money; green means you're ahead.

    1. Tap Pay my share. The amount due is filled in; you can change it.
    2. Pick one of the receiver's payment methods:
      • On a phone: Open UPI, Open Wise, Open PayPal and so on open your own app with the details filled in.
      • On a computer, or paying from someone else's phone: Show QR code and scan it with any UPI app (or your bank's app for SEPA and Pix). The amount is in the code.
      • Zelle, M-Pesa and bank transfers have no link; Bengu shows the steps and Copy details copies everything you need.
      • Paying from another country? Bengu offers Wise, Remitly and Western Union, which send money to an Indian bank account or UPI ID for much less than a bank wire.
    3. After paying, come back to Bengu and record it: the amount, the date, the transaction or UTR number and, ideally, a screenshot.

    If the circle uses Organiser confirms payments, your payment shows as waiting until the organiser taps Received.

    Bengu never holds or moves money. It only opens your payment app and keeps the record.

    7Money trail: proof that bills were paid

    When members send money to one person, the group needs to know that person then paid the hospital, pharmacy or nurse. The Money trail shows this. Open it from the card on the circle's front page.

    What it shows

    • Still with [name]: what the receiver has received, minus what they've paid to providers and paid back to members.
    • Bills paid / Not marked paid: how much of the billed amount has a recorded payment.
    • Needs attention: bills unpaid for more than 7 days, payments with no proof, payments waiting for verification, questioned payments, and money held while bills wait.
    • Three tabs: Unpaid bills, Payments out and the Ledger (every amount in and out with a running balance, downloadable as a PDF statement).

    For the receiver: record a payment to a provider

    1. Tap Mark paid on a bill, or Record a payment to a provider.
    2. Tick the bills this payment covers. One payment can cover several bills or part of one; anything extra is recorded as an advance or deposit.
    3. Enter who you paid, the date, how you paid, the transaction or receipt number, and attach the proof (the hospital's receipt, a UPI screenshot or a bank slip).
    4. Save. The bills now show as paid, waiting to be verified.

    For everyone else: verify or question

    • Open Payments out, tap See proof, and if it matches (same amount, provider and date) tap Verify.
    • If something looks wrong, tap Question it and say why. Everyone sees the question; the receiver can reply and add proof. Once you're satisfied, verify it or withdraw your question.

    The rules

    RuleWhy
    Only the receiver or an organiser can record a payment out.It's their money to account for.
    Someone else must verify it; you can never verify your own.A second pair of eyes on every payment.
    A verified payment is locked. It can still be questioned later.Records can't quietly change.
    A bill can't be marked paid for more than its amount, and a paid bill can't be deleted or reduced.No double counting.
    Every step, including deletions, goes in the activity log.Nothing disappears.
    No app can prove a hospital received money beyond its receipt. That's why each payment needs proof and a second person. For large bills, consider paying the hospital directly (see Who receives the money).

    8Bill Check

    Bill Check looks for signs a bill is wrong, inflated or altered. It runs on its own when a bill is added, and you can run it again with Check this bill.

    Free checks look at the totals, duplicate bill numbers, the same file uploaded twice, dates, prices above the printed MRP, expired medicines, the GST number, and whether the patient name matches other bills. With AI (Bengu Plus) it also reads every line, looks for edited digits or pasted-in sections, and compares prices with what others pay locally and online.

    Bill Check gives warnings to look at, not accusations. Many have innocent explanations. Talk to the provider, and dismiss a warning once it's explained.

    9Reminders

    Each period, Bengu reminds everyone who owes money on WhatsApp and by email, in each person's chosen language. The circle shows when the next reminder goes out.

    • An organiser can send reminders now from the circle, or nudge one person on WhatsApp with Remind on WhatsApp.
    • Each person's reminder language and channels are set on their page under People.
    • Settings → Reminder log shows what was sent and anything that couldn't be (for example a missing number).

    10Insights

    Open Insights from the circle's front page to see:

    • this month, last month and the 3-month average;
    • a chart of the last 12 months, money spent against money paid in;
    • where the money goes, by category, all time or the last 90 days;
    • who has paid what compared with the share each person agreed;
    • top sellers and providers, the largest bills, and record health (receipts attached, open Bill Check warnings, payments waiting).

    Use the download button to export it as a spreadsheet (CSV).

    11Circle agreement

    A short written agreement that everyone signs in the app. It avoids misunderstandings, especially when costs run for months.

    1. The organiser opens Agreement → Write the agreement. The text is built from the circle's settings: purpose, shares, period and due day.
    2. Add your rules: the amount above which a cost is discussed first, when a late payer is reminded, the notice needed to leave, a trusted person to ask if you disagree, and any other terms.
    3. Publish it. Each member reads it, types their full name, ticks I agree and signs.
    4. Download the PDF any time. It lists every signature with its time and a fingerprint of the exact text.

    Changing the agreement publishes a new version, and everyone signs again.

    This is a written record of what your group agreed, accepted electronically. It isn't legal advice; for large sums, ask a lawyer to review it.

    12Statements and exports

    The statement button at the top of the circle makes a statement for any date range, with bills, payments and each person's balance, as a PDF or a spreadsheet (CSV). It's useful for insurance claims, tax records or a family meeting. The receiver's money statement is in the Money trail → Ledger.

    13Bengu Plus and AI

    Everything in this guide is free except AI bill reading and the AI parts of Bill Check, which are Bengu Plus. One Plus plan covers the whole circle, not each person.

    • Right now, AI is free for every circle during early access.
    • When paid plans start, each circle gets 10 free AI reads to try it. After that, bills can still be typed in by hand.
    • Prices depend on your country: for example ₹149 a month or ₹999 a year in India, $3.99 a month in the US, with a 90-day pass for a hospital stay or an event.
    • To get Plus, open Bengu Plus in the circle and tap Request Plus; the Bengu team will contact you about payment.

    14Organiser settings

    • Settings: name, purpose, currency, period, due day, split style, payment confirmation, reminders.
    • People: add or remove members, make someone an organiser, set their reminder language and channels.
    • Archive circle: stops reminders but keeps every record available to everyone.

    15Your account and privacy

    • Change your name, phone, language and notifications in Profile.
    • Delete my account removes your login and personal details. Bills and payments you added stay in your circles, shown as "Former member", so the family's records still add up.
    • Bills, receipts and payments are visible only to members of that circle. Bengu has no ads and never sells data. Read the full privacy policy.

    16Troubleshooting

    What you seeWhat to do

    "No payment methods added yet" on the Pay screen.The organiser needs to add the receiver's payment details: Add payment details.
    The receiver can't record payments to providers.In Who receives the money, link the receiver to the member who uses Bengu (phone icon).
    A bill can't be deleted.It has a recorded payment. Delete that payment record in the Money trail first (only possible before it is verified).
    I can't verify a payment.You recorded it, or the money was yours to pay from. Someone else in the circle has to verify it.
