How it works

Set it up once in about five minutes. After that, Saath keeps the accounts.

Start a circle and invite people

Name it, pick what it's for and the currency, and send the invite code on WhatsApp. People who don't use the app can still be added and reminded.

Agree the shares, add the bills

Each person's share can be any percentage you agree on. Add a bill with a photo or PDF of the receipt and it is split automatically.

Pay directly, see where it went

Members pay the person or hospital you choose with UPI, Wise, PayPal, a bank transfer or cash. The receiver records each bill they pay, with the receipt, and someone else checks it.

Everything a shared budget needs

Built first for one family sharing a parent's care across countries, then opened up to any group.

Fair shares

Percentages, a fixed amount each month, or a different split for one bill. Everyone sees what they owe and why.

Pay any way, no fees taken

Saath never holds your money. It opens the payer's own app with the details filled in, or shows a QR code to scan.

Reminders that aren't awkward

Polite WhatsApp and email reminders go out each period in each person's own language, so nobody has to ask.

Money trail

See what the receiver got, what they paid out, and what is still with them. Every payment to a provider needs proof and is checked by another member.

Bill Check

Flags wrong totals, duplicate bills, prices above the printed MRP, expired medicines and signs of editing. It warns; it never accuses.

Insights

Monthly spending, where the money goes, and who has paid what against the share they agreed.

A written agreement

Put the arrangement in writing (shares, due dates, big costs, leaving) and everyone signs in the app.

Statements

PDF and spreadsheet statements for any dates, ready for an insurer, a tax return or a family meeting.

Made for families far apart

Each person sees amounts in the circle's currency and gets reminders in their language, wherever they live.

Questions

Does Saath hold or move any money?

No. Members pay each other, or the hospital or shop, directly with the apps they already use. Saath opens those apps with the details filled in and keeps the record.

Does everyone need the app?

No. The organiser can add people who never install it; they still get reminders by WhatsApp or email, and can join later and keep their history.

What if the shares aren't equal?

That's normal. Shares can be any percentages the group agrees on, and one bill can be split differently from the rest.

How do we know the money was spent on the bills?

The person who receives the money records each payment they make to a provider, with the receipt. Another member verifies it or asks a question, and the Money trail shows what is still with them.

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything except AI bill reading is free and always will be. AI is free for now too while Saath is in early access; later it becomes part of Saath Plus, priced per circle.
